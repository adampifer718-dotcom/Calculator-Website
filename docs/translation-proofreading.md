# Terms needing native-speaker proofreading

The Italian, European Portuguese and Dutch translations were drafted by AI translation agents on 21 October 2026 and checked automatically. Automated checks cover the structure, the placeholders, metadata length, title uniqueness and identical calculator results. **No human linguistic review has taken place.**

These are the terms the translators marked as uncertain. Please ask a native speaker (ideally one with finance, engineering and medical background) to check them, together with a general read of the pages.

Points that apply to all three languages:
- **US-specific concepts** have no exact local equivalent: sales tax, APR/APY, escrow, HOA, the letter-grade scale and US customary units. They are explained rather than replaced with local systems.
- **Pay periods:** "semi-monthly" (24 per year) and "biweekly" (26 per year) are easily confused in all three languages. Please confirm the chosen wording.
- **Number lists:** in these languages a comma between digits is a decimal mark, so the list help text tells visitors to separate values with semicolons, spaces or new lines.
